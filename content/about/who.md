### Attempts to combine major guild owners in one place have struggled since:
- Guilds come in and out of activity; notability is often ephemeral.
- People come and go, sometimes active, sometimes on hiatus from Wynn.
- Keeping track of the above required activity from a venue's maintainers.

> This system hopes to resolve those factors by automating guild membership and representation.

## What guilds are represented?
> More often than not, major guilds meet multiple of our metrics.

-# Although the specific criteria are a subject of frequent debate, **the hall currently admits the ~35 guilds that meet one or more of the following metrics**:

| Signal | Description |
|---|---|
| **Activity Leaderboards** | We take a guild's activity (i.e. number of players online at any given period of time), average the resulting values, and accept the top 25. |
| **Level Leaderboards** | Any guild that has grinded to guild level 100+ is considered major. |
| **Season Leaderboards** | We admit any guild that has averaged a top 15 placement across the most recent five guild seasons, a top 6 best placement across the past 5 seasons, or a top 5 placement across the past 10 seasons. |
| **War Leaderboards** | Any guild that has a recorded war count of over 50 000. |
| **Raid Leaderboards** | Any guild that is currently top 25 on the global combined raid leaderboard, or top 15 on a leaderboard for a specific graid. |


## What people represent those guilds?
> Four seats per guild with up to one chief per seat: a maximum of four chiefs per guild.

-# Each major guild gets [four seats](/about/what/#available-seats), each of which can be claimed by any person who verifies chiefship in the applicable guild.
**If someone was already in the seat, their predecessor gets kicked from that seat.**
