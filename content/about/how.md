## Seat Allocation
-# Each [major guild](/about/who/#what-guilds-are-represented) is assigned [four seats](/about/what/#available-seats): one ownership contact, one housing contact, one events contact, and one warring contact.
-# **Verified chiefs can claim one or more of their guild's four seats, evicting their predecessor.**

## Claiming Your Seat
> To claim your seat, simply go to [this calculator](https://hall.wynnvets.org/join).

**After warning you about anyone currently occupying your requested seat(s), it will encode your request into a short `HALL##` code.**

**Typing that code into `verify.wynnvets.org` will give you a single-use discord link that will, as you, join the Hall as the contact for your requested seats**
